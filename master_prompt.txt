1. MANDATORY IMAGE FORMAT

Advertising placement: Social Media Feed Ad
Aspect ratio: 4:5 portrait
Final resolution: 1080 × 1350 pixels
Orientation: Vertical
Number of final images: 3 independent outputs

Strict format requirements

* Compose every concept specifically for a 4:5 portrait canvas from the beginning.
* Do not create landscape or square compositions that will later require destructive cropping.
* Maintain a visually balanced vertical composition.
* Keep the product large enough to be immediately recognizable on a mobile screen.
* Protect important product features from the image edges.
* Maintain approximately 5–8% breathing room around important visual details wherever the composition allows.
* Keep the principal product and focal interaction within a central, crop-tolerant area where practical.
* Allow sufficient headroom and lower-frame space for a deliberate professional composition.
* Avoid excessive empty space that makes the product appear small.
* Avoid extreme close-ups that cut off identity-critical product components.
* Design a clear visual hierarchy that remains understandable when viewed as a small mobile-feed image.
* Use negative space only where it strengthens the advertisement.

The entire composition must feel purpose-built for a premium 4:5 feed advertisement.

If the image-generation system cannot output 1080 × 1350 natively, create the closest suitable high-resolution 4:5 image, then fit and resize it to exactly 1080 × 1350 without stretching, deforming, or cropping important product details.

5. FEED AD CREATIVE DIRECTION

Every concept must meet these mobile advertising standards.

Immediate visual impact

The composition should communicate its main visual idea almost instantly while scrolling.

Use:

* A strong focal point
* Clear subject separation
* Purposeful lighting
* Elegant contrast
* Professional colour harmony
* A distinctive but believable creative idea

Product dominance

The actual product must remain the visual hero.

The model, environment, props and decorative elements must support the product rather than compete with it.

Mobile readability

The main product must be recognizable even when displayed at reduced size.

Avoid:

* Tiny product placement
* Excessive background complexity
* Crowded compositions
* Unnecessary visual elements
* Important details placed at the extreme edges
* Excessive wide-angle distortion
* Overly dark or visually muddy scenes

Photographic quality

Aim for:

* High-end commercial realism
* Detailed product textures
* Physically plausible shadows
* Natural contact between objects
* Controlled reflections
* Realistic lighting falloff
* Correct proportions
* Realistic human anatomy
* Refined professional colour grading

Text handling

By default, create text-free advertising masters.

Do not add:

* Invented headlines
* Prices
* Discounts
* Calls to action
* Social-media interface elements
* Watermarks
* Decorative typography
* AI-generated brand names

Preserve existing product branding where visible and relevant.

If promotional text is explicitly supplied later, reserve appropriate space for a separate accurate typography layer.
